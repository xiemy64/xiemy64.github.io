Mengyi Xie

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at Yale University (expected graduation: 2027), advised by Wilhelm Schlag. I received my bachelor’s degree from Nanyang Technological University, where my undergraduate advisor was Tang Wee Kee.

I am interested in analysis and partial differential equations. My current research focuses on the long-time behavior and stability of solutions to nonlinear dispersive equations.

I am on the academic job market for 2026–2027.

Email: mengyi.xie (at) yale.edu
